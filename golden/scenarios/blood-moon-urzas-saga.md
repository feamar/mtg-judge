# Scenario — Blood Moon and Urza's Saga

**Source validation:** VALIDATED

## Judge Call

Blood Moon and Urza's Saga are on the battlefield. What is Urza's Saga now, what happens to its abilities and lore counters, and is it sacrificed?

## Expected Ruling

Urza's Saga is a **nonbasic Enchantment Land — Mountain** while Blood Moon applies. It remains an enchantment and remains a Saga, because Blood Moon changes its land subtype; it does not remove its other card types or its enchantment subtype.

Because it becomes a Mountain through an effect that sets a land to a basic land type, CR 305.7 removes the abilities generated by its rules text and gives it the Mountain intrinsic mana ability. Its printed chapter abilities are therefore gone while Blood Moon applies.

Under the current 2026 Saga rules, this has two important consequences:

- A Saga with no chapter abilities does not receive the normal turn-based lore counter in the precombat main phase.
- A Saga with no chapter abilities is not sacrificed by the Saga-specific state-based action merely for having no chapter abilities.

If Urza's Saga enters while Blood Moon is already applying, the current rules also matter because the Saga's initial lore-counter behavior is intrinsic. Blood Moon can remove that intrinsic ability before it can create the replacement effect, so it can enter with zero lore counters under the current 2026 rules.

If Blood Moon later leaves, Urza's Saga regains its chapter abilities and its Saga machinery can resume from whatever lore-counter state it then has.

## Normative source chain

- Blood Moon Oracle text
- Urza's Saga Oracle text
- CR 305.7 — effect setting a land to a basic land type changes its land types, removes abilities generated by its rules text, and grants the corresponding intrinsic mana ability.
- CR 613.1d — type-changing effects are applied in layer 4.
- CR 714.3a–c — current Saga intrinsic abilities / lore-counter progression.
- CR 714.4 — Saga sacrifice rule applies to a Saga with one or more chapter abilities.
- Relevant state-based-action rule corresponding to Saga sacrifice.

## 2026 rules-version requirement

This scenario is explicitly version-sensitive. Older rulings and release notes may say that an ability-less Urza's Saga is immediately sacrificed. That is no longer the current rule. The corpus must store the rules version used for the expected answer and must not treat historical scenario answers as timeless authority.

## Test-design value

This tests whether the engine can distinguish:
- changing a land subtype from removing unrelated card types/subtypes;
- CR 305.7's ability-removal consequence from an ordinary type-changing effect;
- printed/intrinsic abilities from turn-based and state-based Saga rules;
- current rules from obsolete historical rulings;
- continuous-effect reasoning from state-based-action reasoning.
