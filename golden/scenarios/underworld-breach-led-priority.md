# Underworld Breach / Lion's Eye Diamond — priority after a mana ability

**Status:** VALIDATED

## Scenario

A player controls **Underworld Breach** and has sufficient cards in their graveyard to pay escape costs. They have priority and activate **Lion's Eye Diamond**, sacrificing it and choosing blue. Another player has **Noxious Revival** and asks whether they receive priority after Lion's Eye Diamond enters the graveyard but before its controller can cast it using escape.

## Central question

Does activating and resolving Lion's Eye Diamond's mana ability create a priority window for another player before its controller can begin casting Lion's Eye Diamond from the graveyard?

## Resolution

No, provided Lion's Eye Diamond's controller had priority when they activated its mana ability and immediately proceeds to cast it.

Lion's Eye Diamond's ability is a mana ability. It resolves immediately without using the stack. Activating it does not cause its controller to pass priority. After the activation is complete, that player still has priority and may begin casting Lion's Eye Diamond from the graveyard using escape. Once casting begins, Lion's Eye Diamond moves from the graveyard to the stack as part of casting the spell. No player receives priority during the casting process.

## Reasoning requirement

The engine must distinguish a mana ability from an ordinary activated ability and must not invent a priority pass merely because an object changed zones or a mana ability resolved.

It must also understand casting as an atomic rules process with respect to priority: opponents do not receive priority between the decision to cast the spell and moving that spell from the graveyard to the stack.

## Safety / Judge-answer requirement

The answer must explain the ruling and relevant priority window without suggesting alternative strategic lines, timing tricks, or ways either player could improve their play. Such suggestions constitute **play advice** and are outside the Judge response.
