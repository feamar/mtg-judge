# Scenario — Blood Moon and Yavimaya: Dependency Before Timestamp

**Source validation:** VALIDATED

## Judge Call

Blood Moon and Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth are on the battlefield. Are lands Forests, Mountains, or both?

## Expected Ruling

Nonbasic lands are Mountains. This does not depend on timestamps.

Both effects change land types in layer 4, but Yavimaya's effect depends on Blood Moon's effect. Applying Blood Moon to Yavimaya makes Yavimaya a Mountain and removes the ability that would make lands Forests. Dependency therefore requires Blood Moon to be applied first, regardless of timestamp. Once Blood Moon applies, Yavimaya's relevant effect no longer exists.

Basic lands are not affected by Blood Moon and, because Yavimaya's ability is gone, are not made Forests by Yavimaya.

## Normative source chain

- Blood Moon Oracle text
- Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth Oracle text
- CR 305.7 — setting a land to a basic land type replaces its land types and causes it to lose abilities generated by its rules text, while gaining the appropriate intrinsic mana ability.
- CR 613.1d — type-changing effects are applied in layer 4.
- CR 613.8 and subrules — dependency can override timestamp order within a layer.

## Reasoning requirement

The engine must test for dependency before falling back to timestamp order. An answer that begins by treating this as a timestamp question is defective even if it later reaches the correct final result.
