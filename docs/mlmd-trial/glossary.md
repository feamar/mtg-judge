# Glossary

Imported unchanged from PRD §9 (26 terms), with one change:
- **Policy framework**: one multiplayer addendum (to the MTR and IPG) chosen for an event, or none. MVP 1 options: the MTRA (Notion version) or plain MTR + IPG. (OQ-37, Frank 2026-09-28; replaces "Portuguese Multiplayer Addendum or the MTRA")
- **Unresolved**: a valid result in every context, including play at home: the governing text doesn't settle a needed point, so the judge doesn't rule. Escalation is a separate step, taken only where a human judge is available. It arises only for infractions and for fixing an illegal game state. (Frank, 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06; replaces PRD §9 "escalated rather than decided")
- **Phone app**: the mobile front end; may run on-device or hybrid. One term for "phone app" and "mobile app". (Frank, 2026-10-05)
- **Admin** is the role in the product; **product owner** is the person who decides requirements (Frank). "Owner" alone is not used. (Frank, 2026-10-05)
